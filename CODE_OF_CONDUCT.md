# Code of Conduct

SplineXR was born in a design school, and many of the people around it are students. We want this project to be a welcoming place to learn, ask questions and share work, whatever your level, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners. Every question is a good question.
- Give feedback on the work, never on the person.
- Assume good intentions, and ask before concluding.
- Credit the work of others (scenes, templates, code, ideas).
- Respect people's privacy: no personal information, photos or faces of other people shared without their consent — this matters for a tool used in shared classrooms and headsets.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, intimidation.
- Sexual or violent content, including in example scenes and templates.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions.

## Scope

This code applies to the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and to any space where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If you witness or experience unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through the contact options on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/a-ng-d). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove content, or temporarily or permanently ban a participant, depending on the situation.
